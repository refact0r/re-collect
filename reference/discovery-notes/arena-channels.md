# Are.na channels for the discovery feed (scouted 2026-11-14)

Two research agents checked about 70 channels through the v3 API, as a guest,
about 140 calls in all. The 20 marked **in** are in `SOURCES`
(`src/convex/lib/discoverySources.ts`), split into three groups that each take
one feed slot per round. Taste was judged mostly from block titles, source
domains and some thumbnail grids, so prune whatever doesn't land.

"Good" is the number of Image blocks and Link blocks with an image among the
50 newest blocks; the feed drops everything else.

## Notes for the feed

- **Blank screenshots:** Are.na's screenshots of dark sites and loading
  screens are often all black or all white. About 5 of 16 in
  `interesting-web-design-and-ux` were. A blurhash or luminance filter would
  catch them.
- **GIFs:** a GIF block's `image.small/medium/large` all point at the original
  GIF, so motion channels animate in the feed and keep as GIFs.
- **Source URLs:** UI and motion blocks often link to x.com, Instagram or
  LinkedIn rather than the work itself.
- **Duplicates:** channels overlap (Printer Matter and David Peyote, Sammal
  and the GR channels). The pool dedupes by normalized URL, so an image shows
  once, under whichever channel delivered it first.
- **Dates:** a channel's `updated_at` is not its last add. Use the first
  block's `connection.connected_at` with `sort=created_at_desc`.
- **Finding channels:** Are.na search needs a login and web search found
  almost nothing. Snowballing through `/v3/blocks/<id>/connections` and
  curators' channel lists is what worked.
